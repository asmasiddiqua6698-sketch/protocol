Follow your gastroenterologist's prescribed treatment exactly as directed. A thin slice of ginger steeped in warm water after meals can settle digestion — stop if it increases burning.

Go slow on purpose. With erosions, a sudden jump in fibre or fermented foods can feel like "gut healing gone wrong" when it's simply too much, too fast. Add one food at a time and hold for a few days before the next.

Bringing back fermented foods

Keep plain curd and chaas. Then, one at a time and 3–4 days each, note how you feel in your diary:

  • Idli or dosa — 2 idlis with an egg or chicken side
  • Ragi ambali — a small cup of plain fermented ragi porridge with jeera and salt
  • Dhokla — 2 small steamed pieces, little oil, no chilli tempering
  • Tempeh — with rice or quinoa and vegetables
  • Beetroot-carrot kanji — last, 2–3 tbsp after a meal, never on an empty stomach

If you get burning, regurgitation or bloating within 2 hours, step back. Skip kombucha, vinegar pickles, and kimchi or sauerkraut for now.

Hormones and seed cycling

Seed cycling is a simple food-based habit: rotating seeds across the two halves of your cycle. It is gentle and safe to layer onto this plan. Research on seed cycling itself is limited, but the seeds are nutritious and add fibre, minerals and healthy fats.

Days 1–14From the first day of your period. 1 tbsp ground flaxseed + 1 tbsp raw pumpkin seeds, once daily. Grind flax fresh; add to curd, a smoothie, oats or a salad.
Day 15 until your next period1 tbsp sesame seeds + 1 tbsp sunflower seeds, once daily. Lightly roast or use raw; add to curd, sabzi or a stir-fry.

Irregular or not tracking? Use the moon as an approximate guide: new to full moon = flax + pumpkin; full to new moon = sesame + sunflower.

How to start, given your reflux

  • Start with 1 tsp of each seed and build up over 1–2 weeks.
  • Grind finely and take with a meal, not on an empty stomach.
  • If bloating or burning increases, drop back and go slower.

For painful periods

Fish twice a week, magnesium-rich foods (pumpkin seeds, spinach), regular meals, gentle movement and a heat pack can help. Because you're avoiding painkillers, please tell your doctor if the pain stops you doing daily things, or if bleeding is heavy or has clots.

Why the gut connects. Gut bacteria help break down the fibre and lignans in flax and sesame and help clear used oestrogen, so a calmer, more varied gut supports this habit. Steadier hormones also tend to mean steadier mood, sleep and cravings.

Track for 2–3 cycles: cycle start date, PMS symptoms, mood, skin, energy and sleep.

Blood count, medicines and supplements

Anaemia

Include lean meat, fish, eggs, whole moong, chana, rajma, beans and leafy greens. Keep tea and coffee away from iron-rich meals, and where practical keep the curd apart from your main iron meal too.

Because you've reported anaemia and have stomach findings, the cause should be confirmed. Discuss a repeat CBC, ferritin, iron and TIBC or transferrin saturation with your doctor. If small red cells persist despite adequate iron, discuss thalassaemia-trait testing. Please don't self-start high-dose iron — if haemoglobin is around 10 g/dL, food alone may not be enough.

Medicines

  • Nexito 5 mg (about a year): continue exactly as prescribed and don't stop or change it without your prescribing doctor.
  • Avoid painkillers such as ibuprofen, diclofenac or aspirin for now, unless your doctor says they're safe for you.
  • Don't add St John's wort, 5-HTP or other herbal or serotonergic products without prescriber review.
  • Neuro Forte + CoQ10: keep the product label handy for review.

Supplements

  • Iron: only if deficiency is confirmed; discuss with your doctor because of the erosions and reflux.
  • Vitamin D, B12, folate: check current levels and supplement when indicated.
  • Omega-3: food first — fish twice a week; no routine supplement.
  • Magnesium: prefer food unless a clinician identifies a need.
  • Protein powder: optional if food can't meet your protein needs; choose a simple, low-sugar product without added stimulants.
  • Probiotic supplements: not automatic. "Liver detox" blends are no substitute for nutrition, exercise and medical care.
